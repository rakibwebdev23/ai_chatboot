# APS Chatbot Widget Integration Guide

This guide explains how to integrate the APS Chatbot widget into any website or CMS (WordPress, Shopify, etc.).

## 🚀 Quick Integration

To add the chatbot to your website, copy and paste the following script tag into your website's `<head>` or just before the closing `</body>` tag.

```html
<!-- APS Chatbot Widget -->
<script src="https://office-ai-chatboot.vercel.app/widget.js"></script>
```

---

## 🛠 Step-by-Step CMS Installation

### 1. WordPress
1. Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard.
2. Go to **Appearance** > **Theme File Editor**.
3. On the right side, find and click on `footer.php`.
4. Scroll down to the bottom and find the `</body>` tag.
5. Paste the script tag right above `</body>`.
6. Click **Update File**.

*Alternatively, use a plugin like "Insert Headers and Footers" to safely add the script without editing theme files.*

### 2. Shopify
1. Go to your Shopify Admin.
2. Navigate to **Online Store** > **Themes**.
3. Click the **three dots (...)** next to your active theme and select **Edit Code**.
4. Open the `theme.liquid` file.
5. Scroll to the bottom and locate the `</body>` tag.
6. Paste the script tag just above it.
7. Click **Save**.

### 3. Wix
1. Go to your Wix Dashboard.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Custom Code** (under the Advanced section).
3. Click **+ Add Custom Code**.
4. Paste the script tag in the text box.
5. Set "Place Code in:" to **Body - end**.
6. Click **Apply**.

### 4. Squarespace
1. Go to your Squarespace Home Menu.
2. Click **Settings** > **Advanced** > **Code Injection**.
3. Paste the script tag into the **Footer** section.
4. Click **Save**.

### 5. Duda CMS
1. From your Duda Dashboard, enter the **Site Editor**.
2. In the left side panel, click **Settings**.
3. Click on **Head HTML**.
4. Scroll down to the **Body End HTML** section.
5. Paste the script tag into the text box.
6. Click **Save** and **Republish** your site.
